AI As Peers

Project charter · requirements · peer-to-peer AI framework

Under Active Development

Peer-to-peer AI collaboration framework, the concept is simple, you can create a group chat, in facebook or anywhere, and invice your favorite AIs to join in and have a discussion, everyone gets the the same prompts from the user every one of the AIs can respond. The user can moderate giving the floor to any one of the AIs and manage the discussion.

  • Decentralized inference network
  • Zero-knowledge model sharing
  • Token-incentivized compute
  • Under active development – Q1 2026

Running on Apache Tomcat
