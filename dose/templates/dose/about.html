About PolySaaS – The Universal Admin Console

PolySaaS is the open-source, multi-tenant dashboard that turns every SaaS tool you already pay for into a first-class widget inside your own admin panel.

No APIs. No rate limits. No "coming soon".

Just click a sidebar item and the real tool appears — running natively, with your real data, your real session, your real permissions.

What it does today

  • Full Gmail (inbox, compose, send, search — everything)
  • Grok (xAI) chat
  • GitHub repositories & pull requests
  • Notion pages & databases
  • Airtable bases
  • OSTicket full agent panel
  • + 30 more in active forks (Zendesk, Salesforce, Linear, Stripe, Shopify, Intercom…)

The magic is the proxy + Atomic Services

Every widget is a secure, bi-directional proxy.

Every button on every tool can become an "Atomic Service" — a one-click action from anywhere in PolySaaS.

Example: turn Gmail's "Compose" into a global hot-key, turn OSTicket's "New Ticket" into a widget on every client dashboard, turn Stripe's "New Invoice" into a button in your CRM.

Who it's for

  • Digital agencies (white-label it for every client in 60 seconds)
  • Internal-tools teams tired of Retool licensing
  • Power users who want one login for literally everything

Open-source · MIT license · Self-host or cloud
