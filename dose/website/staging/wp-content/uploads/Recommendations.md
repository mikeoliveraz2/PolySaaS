### WordPress's Built-in AI Assistant
You mentioned seeing WordPress itself as an AI assistant—yes, that's real! If you're on **WordPress.com** (hosted), or using the **Jetpack plugin** on self-hosted WordPress (super common with Astra), you get the **Jetpack AI Assistant** built right into the Gutenberg editor.

- **What it does for you**:
  - Generate full page content (e.g., draft your About page, Contact blurb, or feature descriptions for the sidebar).
  - Suggest titles, summaries, tone adjustments (professional SaaS vibe), grammar fixes, translations.
  - Create structured lists/tables (perfect for your sidebar features/capabilities).
  - Generate images from prompts (e.g., icons for features or hero banners).
  - Build forms (e.g., for Contact Us or Sign Up).

- **How it eases workload**: Type a prompt like "Write a professional About page for PolySaaS, a multi-tenant SaaS orchestration platform with Atomic Services and real-time integrations" → it drafts it instantly. Refine and publish.
- **Free tier**: 20 requests/month. Paid upgrade (~$10-12/month via Jetpack) for unlimited.
- **Suited to your Astra setup?** Yes—Jetpack works seamlessly with Astra. Install Jetpack (free plugin), activate AI Assistant block.

Definitely try this first—it's the "official" WordPress AI you spotted.

### Other Top AI Plugins Particularly Suited to WordPress (and Your Site)
These are lightweight, popular in 2025, and integrate well with Astra/Gutenberg:

1. **AI Engine** (by Meow Apps) – Free + Pro
   Most versatile. Chatbots, content/image generation, forms, playground for testing.
   → Great for adding a site chatbot (e.g., answer questions about PolySaaS features).
   → Generate sidebar bullet lists or gallery descriptions.

2. **Rank Math or AIOSEO** (SEO plugins with AI)
   AI for titles/meta descriptions, content optimization, internal links.
   → Auto-generate SEO-friendly page titles/descriptions for your About, Gallery, etc.—saves tons of time on business site SEO.

3. **SeedProd** (Landing Page Builder with AI)
   Drag-drop + AI to generate full pages/layouts.
   → Quickly build your homepage with sidebar, top bar, hero—AI writes headlines/copy.

4. **Spectra** (Astra's own free page builder blocks) – Has some AI integrations emerging, but pair with Jetpack for full power.

From the Astra companion plugins you have (like Hooks, Bulk Edit), none are AI-heavy, but they pair perfectly with these.
