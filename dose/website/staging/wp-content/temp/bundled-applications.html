Odoo is a business management software suite developed in Belgium. It includes modules for customer relationship management, e-commerce, billing, accounting, manufacturing, warehouse operations, project management, and inventory management.

SuiteCRM is a powerful, free, and open-source Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform, developed by SalesAgility and built as a fork of SugarCRM, offering businesses a flexible, enterprise-grade solution to manage sales, marketing, and customer service without vendor lock-in or high licensing fees, providing full control over data and extensive customization through self-hosting or managed services. It provides a 360-degree view of customers, automating processes like lead conversion, campaign management, and reporting, making it a popular, cost-effective alternative to proprietary CRMs. 

Nextcloud is a free, open-source, self-hosted platform that acts like a private Google Drive or Dropbox, giving you control over your files, data, and collaboration tools. It allows users to store, sync, and share files, calendars, contacts, and photos, while offering features like video calls, chat, and document editing, all accessible via web, desktop, or mobile apps. Its core strength is data sovereignty, letting individuals and businesses keep their sensitive information on their own servers.  

Gmail is Google's free, popular web-based email service, offering secure cloud storage for messages, powerful search, and integration with other Google tools like Drive, Calendar, and Meet, accessible via web, app, or other clients, known for strong spam filtering and user-friendly features like labels and customizable themes, serving as a core part of Google Workspace for personal and business use. 

PolySysMon is a system monitoring tool that lets you collect information about literally every aspect of your business from the health of your hardware to the status of accounts and collect and organize the data for interpretatio, trend analysis and alerting of events.

Monitor Logger is a special kind of monitor as it just watches logs for anything that meets your criteria, Error logs, system logs, access logs, all can be reead and data extracted and alerts generated.

Dolibarr is an open-source, modular ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) and CRM (Customer Relationship Management) software for businesses and foundations, designed for easy management of company activities, from sales/CRM (quotes, orders, invoices) to finance, projects, and inventory, available for self-hosting or cloud use, offering customization through modules to fit small to large organizations. It's built on PHP, supports various databases, and includes features for human resources, e-commerce, and external integrations like payment gateways (PayPal, Stripe) and social media. 

A Celery application is a Python-based system for handling asynchronous tasks, offloading heavy work (like sending emails, processing images, data analysis) from a main application (Django, Flask) to background workers, using a message broker (Redis, RabbitMQ) for communication, ensuring web apps stay responsive and fast, and allowing for scalable, distributed task processing. 

Mattermost is an open-source, self-hostable collaboration platform offering secure messaging, file sharing, and workflow automation for teams, especially in high-stakes environments like defense, government, and finance, acting as a customizable alternative to Slack/Teams with focus on data sovereignty, security, and control, running on-prem, private cloud, or edge. It provides features for DevSecOps, incident response, and mission operations, supporting AI integration and integrations with other tools. 

WordPress is a powerful, free, open-source Content Management System (CMS) used to build and manage websites and blogs, powering over 40% of the internet by offering a visual dashboard, themes for design, plugins for features, and flexibility for users from beginners to developers, with two main paths: self-hosted WordPress.org or the managed WordPress.com. It started in 2003 as a blogging tool but has grown into a versatile platform for e-commerce, portfolios, forums, and more, relying on PHP and MySQL.
