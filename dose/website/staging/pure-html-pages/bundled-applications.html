Odoo is a business management software suite developed in Belgium. It includes modules for customer relationship management, e-commerce, billing, accounting, manufacturing, warehouse operations, project management, and inventory management.

SuiteCRM is a powerful, free, and open-source Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform, developed by SalesAgility and built as a fork of SugarCRM, offering businesses a flexible, enterprise-grade solution to manage sales, marketing, and customer service without vendor lock-in or high licensing fees.

Nextcloud is a free, open-source, self-hosted platform that acts like a private Google Drive or Dropbox. It allows users to store, sync, and share files, calendars, contacts, and photos, while offering features like video calls, chat, and document editing.

Gmail is Google's free, popular web-based email service, offering secure cloud storage for messages, powerful search, and integration with other Google tools like Drive, Calendar, and Meet.

PolySysMon is a system monitoring tool that lets you collect information about literally every aspect of your business from hardware health to account status, organize the data for interpretation, trend analysis, and alerting of events.

Monitor Logger watches logs for anything that meets your criteria — error logs, system logs, access logs — extracting data and generating alerts.

Dolibarr is an open-source, modular ERP and CRM, designed for easy management of company activities from sales and invoicing to projects and inventory, available for self-hosting or cloud use.

Celery is a Python-based system for handling asynchronous tasks, offloading heavy work (like sending emails, processing images, data analysis) to background workers using a message broker (Redis, RabbitMQ).

Mattermost is an open-source, self-hostable collaboration platform offering secure messaging, file sharing, and workflow automation for teams — a customizable alternative to Slack/Teams.

WordPress is a powerful, free, open-source Content Management System (CMS) used to build and manage websites and blogs, powering a large portion of the internet.
