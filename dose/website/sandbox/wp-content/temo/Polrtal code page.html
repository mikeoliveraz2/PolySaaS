The PolySaaS Portal

Your single front door to every tool and service — internal or external.

Portlets

The original portal concept: self-contained mini-apps that show live data or functionality directly on your dashboard. PolySaaS will support standard portlet specifications so you can use any compliant component.

Custom Links

Add one-click shortcuts to internal PolySaaS tools or external SaaS apps. Admins can push tenant-wide links; individual users can add personal ones. Everything you need is always one click away.

Widgets & Gadgets

Lightweight, glanceable components — clocks, metrics, task lists, feeds, or custom data. Drag, drop, resize — make your portal feel like home.
