Architecture

PolySaaS Architecture has a solid foundation built on the following principles:

  • Service Oriented
  • Microservices
  • Message Oriented Middleware
  • Data Driven
  • Change behavior by changing data
  • Low or No Code
  • Loosely Coupled
  • Separation of Concerns
  • Multi-Tenant
  • Google Cloud Platform
  • BigQuery
  • Docker on Kubernetes
  • PubSub
  • Cloud Storage

The resulting architecture is like the sturdy foundation and flexible frame of a house. Imagine building a home where you can easily add rooms, move walls, or expand without tearing everything down. It’s designed to grow with your needs—starting small but ready to handle more users, features, or data without breaking. This means your business can change and evolve quickly, like swapping furniture in a room instead of rebuilding the whole place. No crashes or major rewrites; just smooth, reliable upgrades.

The Atomic Services Story

PolySaaS Atomic Services are like tiny, self-contained tools in a toolbox, each designed to do one specific job really well. Imagine a screwdriver for tightening screws or a hammer for nailing—nothing extra, just the essentials. These services can be used alone or combined with others, making it easy to mix and match for your needs, like building a custom gadget from Lego blocks. They keep things fast and simple, so you can swap or add them without messing up the whole system.

Why are they important

Atomic Services matter because they force your architecture to stay clean, composable, and scalable —and for someone like you who’s building multi‑tenant SaaS with microservices, they’re one of the most powerful design principles you can lean on.
