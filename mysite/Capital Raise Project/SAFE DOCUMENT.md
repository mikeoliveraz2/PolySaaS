### Draft SAFE Document (YC Template)

Below is a **draft SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)** based on the **Y Combinator standard template** (post-money version with valuation cap and discount, ideal for pre-seed SaaS raises like yours). This is **free and customizable**—it's not legal advice, so **consult a lawyer** before using.

> **Key Notes for Your $15k Raise:**
> - **Valuation Cap:** $4M (adjust based on negotiations).
> - **Discount:** 20% (standard for MVPs with traction).
> - **Pro Rata:** Optional—include if angels want rights.
> - **File as PDF:** Use Google Docs → Export. Sign via DocuSign.
> - **Source:** Official YC template (public domain for startups).

**[Your SaaS Company Name]**
**SIMPLE AGREEMENT FOR FUTURE EQUITY**

**Date:** [Insert Date, e.g., November 12, 2025]

**Investor:** [Angel Name or "Syndicate Lead"], an individual / [Entity Name], a [corporation / limited liability company / etc.] (the “Investor”)

**Company:** [Your SaaS Company Name], a [Delaware / your state] [corporation / limited liability company / etc.] (the “Company”)

**A.** The Company is presently conducting a private offering of its securities (the “Offering”) pursuant to the operative documents for such Offering.

**B.** The Investor desires to make an investment in the Company by purchasing a Simple Agreement for Future Equity (a “SAFE”) for an aggregate investment amount of [e.g., $15,000] (the “Purchase Amount”).

**C.** The Investor and the Company are executing this instrument (this “SAFE”) in reliance upon exemption from securities registration afforded by Section 4(a)(2) of the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), and the regulations promulgated thereunder, or any successor provisions, rules or regulations thereto, and the provisions of state securities or “blue sky” laws, which exempt transactions by issuers with no or few public investors from registration.

**D.** The Investor acknowledges that neither the offer nor sale of this SAFE has been registered under the Securities Act or any applicable state securities laws in reliance on the exemptions described in Recital C, and that the Company’s reliance on such exemptions is based in part on the representations made by the Investor in this SAFE.

**E.** The Investor is acquiring this SAFE for investment only and not with a view to, or for sale in connection with, any distribution thereof, and the Investor has no present intention of selling or distributing the same.

**F.** The Investor has the financial ability to bear the economic risk of his, her, its or their investment, including the total loss thereof, and has sufficient knowledge and experience in business and financial matters to be able to evaluate the risks involved in the investment.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the foregoing recitals and the mutual promises, covenants and conditions set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

**1. SAFE.** This SAFE is intended to be a simple agreement for future equity and is not a debt instrument or other evidence of indebtedness of the Company.

**2. Purchase Amount.** The Investor shall pay the Purchase Amount to the Company in immediately available funds in accordance with the instructions provided by the Company.

**3. Equity.**
(a) **Conversion.** This SAFE shall convert into shares of the Company’s [preferred stock / common stock] (the “Preferred Stock”) upon the occurrence of a Qualified Financing (as defined below).
(b) **Qualified Financing.** A “Qualified Financing” means the next sale of equity securities of the Company (other than this SAFE) in a single transaction or series of related transactions pursuant to which the Company receives gross proceeds of at least $[e.g., 1,000,000] (a “Qualified Financing”).
(c) **Valuation Cap.** The per share price for the Preferred Stock issuable upon conversion of this SAFE shall be the lower of: (i) the price per share of the Preferred Stock calculated based on the [post-money / pre-money] valuation of $[4,000,000] (the “Valuation Cap”), or (ii) the price per share of the Preferred Stock calculated based on the [20]% discount to the price per share of the Preferred Stock in the Qualified Financing (the “Discount Price”).
(d) **Number of Shares.** The number of shares of Preferred Stock issuable upon conversion of this SAFE shall be the quotient obtained by dividing the Purchase Amount by the price per share determined pursuant to Section 3(c).
(e) **Pro Rata Rights.** [Include if applicable: The Investor shall have the right, but not the obligation, to purchase a pro rata portion of any securities sold in the Qualified Financing on the same terms and conditions as offered to other investors.]

**4. Termination.**
(a) **Liquidity Event.** If a Liquidity Event (as defined below) occurs prior to the conversion of this SAFE, the Company shall pay to the Investor, in cash, an amount equal to the greater of: (i) the Purchase Amount, or (ii) the amount the Investor would receive if this SAFE converted into shares of the Company’s common stock immediately prior to the Liquidity Event and such shares were then sold for the consideration paid in the Liquidity Event.
(b) **Liquidity Event Definition.** A “Liquidity Event” means a sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company or a merger, consolidation or other business combination in which the equity holders of the Company immediately prior to the transaction do not hold a majority of the equity interests in the surviving entity.
(c) **Dissolution.** If the Company dissolves or winds up before this SAFE converts or is terminated, the Investor shall receive a pro rata portion of the assets remaining after payment of liabilities, if any.

**5. Representations and Warranties of the Company.** The Company represents and warrants to the Investor that:
(a) It is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of incorporation.
(b) It has full power and authority to enter into this SAFE and perform its obligations hereunder.
(c) This SAFE constitutes a valid and binding obligation of the Company, enforceable in accordance with its terms.
(d) The execution and delivery of this SAFE do not conflict with any agreement to which the Company is a party.

**6. Representations and Warranties of the Investor.** The Investor represents and warrants to the Company that:
(a) It is an “accredited investor” as defined in Rule 501(a) of Regulation D promulgated under the Securities Act.
(b) It has sufficient knowledge and experience in financial and business matters to evaluate the merits and risks of this investment.
(c) It is acquiring this SAFE for investment purposes only and not with a view to distribution.
(d) It understands that this SAFE is a high-risk investment and that it may lose the entire Purchase Amount.

**7. Miscellaneous.**
(a) **Governing Law.** This SAFE shall be governed by the laws of the State of [Delaware / your state], without regard to conflict of laws principles.
(b) **Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and delivered via email or certified mail to the addresses provided.
(c) **Successors and Assigns.** This SAFE shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their successors.
(d) **Entire Agreement.** This SAFE constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.
(e) **Amendments.** No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.
(f) **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall continue in full force.
(g) **Counterparts.** This SAFE may be executed in counterparts, including electronically.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the parties have executed this SAFE as of the date first above written.

### Customization Tips for AngelList
- **Insert Your Details:** Replace placeholders (e.g., company name, amounts) with your specifics. For a $15k raise, ensure the Purchase Amount matches the check size.
- **Version Choice:** This is **post-money SAFE** (YC's preferred for simplicity). If pre-money needed, swap in Section 3(c).
- **Add Schedule:** Attach Exhibit A for investor info if multiple angels.
- **Legal Review:** Use Clerky or Carta for automated generation ($99+), or free tools like Rocket Lawyer. File with your state if required.
