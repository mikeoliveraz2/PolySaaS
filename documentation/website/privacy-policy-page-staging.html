Last updated: September 16, 2026

PolySaaS Online (“PolySaaS,” “we,” “us”) operates the website and platform at polysaas.online and related app hosts (including app.prod-polysaas.cloud). This policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our marketing site or use the PolySaaS service.

How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and improve PolySaaS

Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Service providers (hosting, email, payments, analytics) under contract

We do not sell personal information.

Data location and retention

We host production infrastructure with our current providers (including Hostinger / Dokploy deployments for the app stack). We retain account and operational data for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal, security, and accounting purposes.
