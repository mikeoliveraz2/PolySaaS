HubSpot CRM Inside PolySaaS

HubSpot gives your teams a familiar CRM workspace for contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and task-driven follow-up. In PolySaaS, it becomes more than a standalone CRM: it becomes a connected peer that can share data, trigger orchestration, and feed AI-assisted workflows across your stack.

Each tenant connects its own HubSpot portal. PolySaaS keeps the connection tenant-scoped, surfaces live CRM context in the portal, and lets your operators work in either a direct HubSpot interface or a native PolySaaS view depending on the task.

Sales and customer records stay visible

Give teams one place to work with contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and tasks without losing the structure they already expect from HubSpot.

Tenant-scoped OAuth connection

Every PolySaaS tenant connects its own HubSpot account, so data isolation, token storage, and app state remain tied to the tenant that owns the relationship.

User Context Manager portlets

Surface live HubSpot objects in a prioritized, drag-reorderable PolySaaS dashboard so people can see the right CRM slices without opening every native screen first.

Cross-application orchestration

Use HubSpot events and records as triggers for broader workflows: create follow-up tasks, notify Mattermost, align billing context, or route actions into other bundled applications.

Two ways to work with HubSpot

1. Native HubSpot experience through PolySaaS

For users who need the full CRM interface, PolySaaS can open the HubSpot web UI inside the platform shell so navigation, surrounding context, and future orchestration hooks stay in one place.

2. Focused CRM context inside PolySaaS

For fast review and prioritization, the HubSpot User Context Manager exposes targeted CRM portlets directly in PolySaaS, reducing context switching and helping operators act from a smaller, cleaner surface.

What makes this different

PolySaaS does not just link out to HubSpot. It treats HubSpot as a first-class bundled application that participates in shared authentication, shared tenant management, and shared orchestration design.

What your team gets

  • CRM visibility without fragmentation: sales and support context can sit alongside the rest of your operational stack.
  • Safer multi-tenant operation: each tenant owns its own HubSpot OAuth relationship and data scope.
  • Operational flexibility: use the full HubSpot UI when needed, or smaller purpose-built context panels when that is faster.
  • Better orchestration opportunities: customer events in HubSpot can become inputs to workflows that reach other PolySaaS applications.

Example use cases

  • Pipeline follow-up: when a deal reaches a key stage, notify the right team in Mattermost and launch a downstream workflow.
  • Customer onboarding: align CRM status with portal setup, internal tasks, and provisioning steps across the broader platform.
  • Support escalation: expose ticket context in PolySaaS so operators can correlate CRM records with activity in other bundled apps.
  • Executive review: give stakeholders a lighter-weight CRM summary through PolySaaS portlets instead of full-screen navigation for every check-in.
