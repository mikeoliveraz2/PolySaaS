## Slide 2: The Problem
- Mid-market & enterprise teams run **100–300+ SaaS apps** → sprawl, integration debt, fragmented data
- iPaaS (Zapier, etc.) = point-to-point automation, not deep orchestration for self-hosted stacks
- **No platform delivers visible, collaborative AI teammates** in shared channels
- Cost: inefficiency, compliance risk, vendor lock-in — especially in regulated / sovereignty-sensitive sectors (manufacturing, finance, government)

*Visual:* Icon or stat: “100–335 apps per org” or “$375B+ SaaS market, still fragmented”

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
- **PolySaaS** = one subscription that bundles and orchestrates enterprise open-source apps (Nextcloud, Odoo, Mattermost, Liferay CE, WordPress, etc.)
- **Atomic Services** — Event-driven connectors (Pub/Sub + BigQuery), real-time cross-app workflows
- **PolySniffer** — Auto-discovers APIs, generates proxy handlers, zero-code integration
- **AI As Peers** — Grok, Claude, Gemini as visible “teammates” in Mattermost (reason, draft, code, act)
- **Apps As Peers** (roadmap) — Apps collaborate autonomously (e.g. Odoo → Mattermost → AI → WordPress)
- Self-hostable-capable, GDPR-ready; preserves Liferay/Django investments

*Visual:* Simple diagram: apps → PolySaaS layer → AI peers + orchestration

## Slide 13: Risks & Mitigations
- **Partner activation:** Pilot incentives, joint demos, success-based onboarding
- **Multi-AI cost:** Usage-based pricing, caching, fallback to cheaper models
- **GCP overrun:** Google for Startups application (up to $350K), monitoring from day 1
- **Security/compliance:** Pen-test, SOC 2 Type 1 target, GCP data-residency
- **Competition:** Patent (PolySniffer + AI-peer pattern), emphasize self-hosted/open-core
- **Onboarding churn:** Partner playbook, video library, ACV ≥30 users
