Dynamic Orchestration Explained

Dynamic Orchestration is the core of PolySaaS. It intelligently coordinates actions across your tools in real time.

Any event in any passthrough application — whether internal to our platform or external like Salesforce.com, NetSuite, Odoo, or Mattermost — can trigger dynamic orchestration and AI responses.

Examples:

  • An Odoo invoice update automatically notifies the team in Mattermost with AI-generated insights.
  • A Salesforce deal stage change can trigger workflows across multiple tools.

Value Proposition: Odoo & Mattermost on PolySaaS vs Commercial Hosting Options

Odoo Integration & Dynamic Orchestration

Traditional Path: You can run Odoo Community self-hosted (free software) or pay for Odoo.sh / Odoo Online / Enterprise hosting. Commercial options quickly get expensive:

  • Odoo.sh starts around $70–$300+/month depending on users, workers, storage, and staging environments, plus Enterprise licenses that scale with users.
  • Full managed hosting from third parties adds similar or higher costs with limited customization.
  • Self-hosting saves on licenses but demands significant DevOps effort for maintenance, backups, scaling, security updates, and custom integrations.

PolySaaS Advantage: Instead of expensive Odoo customizations or locked-in commercial hosting, PolySaaS runs on top of your existing Odoo instance (self-hosted Community or Enterprise). You keep full control and avoid vendor lock-in or high per-user licensing.

PolySaaS adds powerful AI-driven Dynamic Orchestration across your entire tool stack — no heavy custom development required. Events in Odoo (invoice updates, deal changes, inventory movements) automatically trigger intelligent actions in Mattermost, Nextcloud, or other tools with AI-generated insights.

Result: You get enterprise-grade automation and integration at a fraction of the cost and complexity of traditional Odoo.sh/Enterprise custom work or multiple paid add-ons.

AI Peers in Mattermost

Traditional Path: Self-hosted Mattermost Team/Entry Edition is free but limited. Unlocking Professional or Enterprise features requires paid licenses (seat-based annual subscriptions, often starting meaningfully at 10+ users). Commercial/cloud hosting options from Mattermost or third parties add further costs for advanced features, support, and scaling.

PolySaaS Advantage: Turn your existing Mattermost (self-hosted preferred) into an intelligent command center with built-in AI Peers — no need for multiple expensive third-party bots or premium Mattermost add-ons.

PolySaaS AI peers understand context across your entire platform (including Odoo events) and handle orchestration, notifications, and workflows natively.

Result: Richer collaboration and automation without stacking licensing fees or managing separate bot services.
