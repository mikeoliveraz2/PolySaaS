The Problem

Every business is drowning in disconnected SaaS.

🧩

SaaS Sprawl

The average small-to-medium business runs 15–25+ SaaS tools — CRM, accounting, chat, support, marketing — each one a silo.

🔌

Fragile Point-to-Point

Existing "automation" tools wire tools together one Zap at a time — brittle, per-task priced, and invisible until something breaks.

🚪

The Lock-In Trap

The alternative — migrate everything into one vendor's all-in-one suite — means disruption, retraining, and permanent lock-in. Most businesses simply won't do it.

The Pivot

We stopped asking customers to switch tools.

We started by building our own hosted business apps — ERP, team chat, file storage. They work, and they're still valuable. But that's not the wedge.

  • Customers don't want to migrate off tools they already trust
  • The real value is connecting what they already run
  • Bundled apps become a cost-effective starting point — not the pitch

The pitch now:

"Keep every tool you already use. We'll connect it, and orchestrate across all of it, automatically."

The Solution

One platform. Every app you already use.

Connect

Link your existing CRM, accounting, chat, and support tools in minutes — no data migration, no downtime.

Orchestrate

AI-driven rules act across every connected tool automatically — an invoice paid in one app can notify your team in another, with zero custom code.

Control Tower

See everything happening across every connected tool in one unified activity feed — not scattered across 20 different logins.

Business Model

BYO costs us less — pricing reflects that.

Bundled / Hosted

We provision and run the app for the customer. Higher cost to us (compute, storage), priced accordingly — the accessible starting point for a category the customer doesn't have covered yet.

Connect (BYO)

Customer already pays for and runs the tool — we only charge for orchestration and AI, not hosting. Materially lower cost to us, and the tier we expect to dominate as the connector library grows.
