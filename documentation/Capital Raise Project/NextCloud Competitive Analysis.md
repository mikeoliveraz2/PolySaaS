Key Insights
Free Tiers: NextCloud's "Simple Signup" program offers 2-5 GB free with providers like The Good Cloud or IONOS (upgrades from €2.90/mo). Ideal for testing, but limited for production.
Budget Options ($2-10/mo): VPS providers like Contabo or Kamatera are cheapest for small teams. Good for 1-5 users, but require manual setup.
Managed Hosting ($10-40/mo): Hostinger or VeeroTech for hands-off setup, backups, and support. Scales to 10+ users.
Enterprise ($50+/user/year): NextCloud's own plans for 100+ users, with advanced features like collaboration tools.
