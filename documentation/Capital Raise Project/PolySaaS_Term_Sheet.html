3 · Conversion & Liquidity

  • Conversion: SAFE converts to preferred stock automatically upon the Company's next Qualified Financing, at the lower of the Valuation Cap price or the discounted price (if a discount applies).
  • Liquidity Event: If the Company is acquired or dissolves before conversion, Investor receives the greater of the Purchase Amount back or the as-converted value, per standard SAFE terms.
  • No interest, no maturity date — a SAFE is not debt. It carries no interest rate and does not need to be repaid if no qualifying event occurs.
